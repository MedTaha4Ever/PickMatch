# PickMatch privacy policy

*Last updated: 27 November 2026*

PickMatch works entirely on your computer.

- **No data leaves your device.** PickMatch does not upload, send or share your photos, file names or any other
  information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- **What it reads:** only the folders you choose (your photos and your client's picks), to compare the pictures and
  copy the originals you confirm.
- **What it writes:** the copies you ask for, the renamed client pictures (if you turn that option on), and a small
  settings file on your computer (last photos folder and your copy options).
- **Text recognition** (reading file names written on pictures) uses the recognition built into Windows, or
  Tesseract on macOS/Linux, and runs on your computer.
- **Links:** the "Support PickMatch" link opens a Wise payment page in your browser. Anything you do there is covered
  by Wise's own privacy policy.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/MedTaha4Ever/PickMatch/issues
